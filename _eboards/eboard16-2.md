### Peer educators

There's been some confusion on the types of peer educators for CSC-151,
their roles and responsibilities, and the names we associate with each.

* **Mentors**. The peer educators who help out during classes .
    * **Mentor Sessions**. The sessions they run twice per week. These
      sessions are intended to help you better master the material and
      prepare for LAs.
* **Evening Tutors**. The peer educators who hang out in this room or 3815
  Sunday through Thursday night. They can help with homework and answer
  questions about various topics.
    * **Evening Tutor Hours**. The times they are here.
* **Individual Tutors**. Peer educators who meet with individual students
  or small groups once per week to go over whatever those students need
  help with. _We generally won't assign individual tutors unless (a)
  you ask for one, (b) you've been attending mentor sessions and turning
  in work, and (c) you meet with SamR._
