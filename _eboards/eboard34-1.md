### AI on reading responses

I've now talked to most of the people who used AI on reading responses and
failed to cite. Uniformly, they agreed that they should get a zero on the
reading response. They were mixed on whether the results should go beyond
that. I had already promised not to report the issue, treating it as a
misunderstanding of course policies. It appears that my decision was 
particularly important for at least one student who was already on academic
probation.

Many indicated that they thought they had to get the correct answer in 
order to receive credit. I believe I had indicated that all I cared about
was the attmept. It seems like everyone understands that now.

Although I had many ideas about appropriate reactions, including capping
final grades at B+ and going back and looking for AI usage in past submissions,
I will leave this as a warning. Don't use AI. Cite help.

### Apparent copying on learning assessments

Some of Friday's LAs appear to involve copying of solutions (most likely
based on knowledge / solutions from earlier sections). I could tell because
I changed some of the LAs for the afternoon section (and those who took
extra LAs during the afternoon section).

I don't know whether it's that people read the earlier LAs and then
prepared an answer for when they showed up later in the day, or
whether a friend wrote an answer on their cheat sheet and someone
else copied fro the cheat sheet, or what. What I do know is that
the "solution" did not match the question.

I've reported the ones I noticed. Having noticed some, I find myself
wondering how many people are doing this. I expect most of you aren't;
I see lots of you trying hard each week. In any case, the loss of faith
in my students is one of the major effect of this cheating.

More significantly, it makes me question whether I can continue my
student-friendly practices, including permitting cheat sheets, allowing
students to take makeups during any of the class times, and permitting
multiple redos. 

Doing something that negatively impacts your classmates, particularly 
to the level that this may, violates self governance. I hope those of
you who cheated will reflect on that impact. (I feel like a broken
record saying this.)

I do not yet know how I'm going to change my quiz procedures.
Fortunately, we don't have quizzes this week, so I'm going to use
Turkey Break to figure it out.
